FAQ

Questions, answered

How does pricing work?

Create a free account and top up your wallet. Each answered call is charged at ₹10 per minute, billed by the second. Unanswered and busy calls are free. Your dashboard shows your balance and what every campaign cost, and calling pauses automatically if your balance runs low.

What should my Excel file contain?

A column with names and a column with phone numbers. Numbers without a country code are treated as Indian (+91). Any other columns, such as city or product interest, are shared with the AI to personalise each call.

Which languages can the AI speak?

English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Bengali and Gujarati. You choose the starting language, and the AI can switch if the customer prefers another.

How long does a campaign take?

Calls start within minutes. Several calls run at the same time, so a list of a few hundred leads usually finishes within a few hours.

Do I have to keep the page open?

No. Calls continue in the background. Log in any time within 24 hours to see results and download your report.

What happens to my data?

We keep your account, wallet and last-used business details so the next campaign is quicker to set up. Your lead lists and call results are never saved to disk and are removed 24 hours after your last visit, so download your report before then.
